Visual Texts and Non-Linearity

The Torah and the Mishnah are non-linear texts. This means that the flow of ideas does not necessarily follow the flow of the laws or narrative. The ideas are presented in blocks and the flow is from block to block.

Block of text

A block of text can be a single verse or a group of related verses. The technical term for a block of verses is "pericope." The flow of a linear text can be visualized like this:

P1>P2>P3>P4>P5>P6etc.        

But not all of our texts are linear. Sometimes it is organized in pairs of pericopes and the conceptual flow is from pair to pair:

P1P2>P3P4>P5P6>P7P8etc.
